Prompt Lab checks your subscription with our billing server and remembers a successful result on each device.

  • A device re-checks your subscription once 6 hours have passed since its last check, including in a tab that stays open, and whenever you choose Refresh Status.
  • A cancellation that takes effect, a full refund or a dispute can therefore take up to 6 hours to appear on a device that already shows Pro. Refresh Status applies it immediately.
  • A new purchase appears as soon as you choose Sync Purchase or refresh.
  • If our billing server cannot be reached, Pro stays available on that device for up to 7 days after its last successful check. After that, Pro pauses on that device until a check succeeds. Nothing is canceled or charged because of this.
  • If we temporarily turn billing off, access a device already has is kept, and Free accounts can use every feature while purchases are unavailable.

Your prompts and library stay on your device and remain available on the Free plan. Data you organized with a Pro feature, such as collections, is kept, but the Pro feature itself is unavailable until you subscribe again.
